Debts Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Debts is developed by Sajarin M. Contact: sajarinm@gmail.com.

Debts helps you keep a private record of person-to-person debts. You can use its core features offline without creating an account. The developer does not operate a server that receives your Ledger. Debts contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting integration.

Information on your device

Debts stores the People and financial records you enter on your device. These can include names, optional phone numbers, amounts, dates, notes, Starting Balances, Settlements, and the history of edited or voided Entries. It also stores your app preferences, reminder templates, Follow-up intentions and History, and Backup status. This information is used to display and calculate your Ledgers and to provide the features you choose. Debts does not import your address book or read your SMS messages.

Optional Google Drive Backup

If you connect a Google account and enable Drive Backup, Debts uploads copies of your Ledger to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. A Backup includes names, optional phone numbers, financial records, notes, revision history, Reminder templates, Currency, and Follow-up intentions and History in the Ledger. Backup metadata includes creation time, format and schema versions, and the uploading phone's name. The connected account email is stored on your device and shown in the app.

Debts requests Google's permission to manage only its own hidden app-data folder. It does not request permission to browse unrelated Drive files. Google handles account authorization; Debts does not receive your Google password.

After you enable automatic Backup, uploads can run when you leave the app, periodically in the background, or when you select Back up now. After verifying a new upload, Debts keeps the newest three Backups, the newest Backup from each of the last seven days and each of the last four weeks, and each phone's newest Backup for four weeks. These periods count back from the newest Backup using Google's storage time; the last Backups of a Ledger that stops changing can remain longer. Older copies outside these rules are deleted. Backup timing depends on connectivity, device scheduling and successful uploads; recent changes may not yet be backed up.

Data is transferred to Google over HTTPS. Backup files are not encrypted by Debts and are not end-to-end encrypted. Google's handling of the account, storage and service metadata is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to your Drive Backup through a developer-operated service.

Debts' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Exports and reminders

You can save a Backup file or export a Person's Statement. These files contain the information you choose to export. Backup files are not encrypted by Debts. Keep them in a destination you trust.

If you choose a sharing action, Statement, Backup or reminder data is handed to the selected app or storage provider. SMS and WhatsApp reminders open an editable draft or recipient picker; Debts does not send the message automatically. The receiving service's privacy policy applies to its use of the information. The developer does not receive a copy through Debts.

Optional private Follow-ups remind you on this device to review a Person's Ledger. Debts stores the Follow-up date and History in the Ledger and uses Android's local notification scheduling. Notifications are not sent to the other Person. You can choose whether a notification preview shows their name; that detail may be visible wherever your device displays notification previews. Notification permission and preview preferences belong to this device and are not restored from a portable Ledger Backup.

App Lock and Android Backup

Optional App Lock uses your device's authentication facilities. Debts does not receive or store your biometric templates or device PIN. App Lock protects access through the app's interface; it does not encrypt exported files or the Ledger database.

Android may back up or transfer app data according to your device and Google Backup settings. Debts excludes its device-bound App Lock preference and internal Restore safety snapshots from this operating-system Backup. On a restored device, you must enable App Lock again deliberately. Operating-system Backup is separate from Debts' optional in-app Drive Backup.

Retention and deletion

Your Ledger remains on your device until you delete its People and associated records, clear app storage, or uninstall Debts. Voiding an Entry or archiving a Person preserves its history rather than deleting it. Editing an Entry preserves the previous financial state.

To stop Drive uploads, use Disconnect Google Drive. You can choose Disconnect and delete backups to remove Debts' stored Drive Backups. Deletion needs a working connection and must complete successfully. Disconnecting without deleting leaves existing Backups in Drive. Deleting a Person or uninstalling Debts does not immediately delete older Drive Backups or files you exported.

You are responsible for deleting exported files and information already sent to another service. Android Backup data is controlled through the device and Google Backup settings. The developer cannot remotely erase your local Ledger or files in your accounts.

If you email the developer for support, the developer receives the information you include and uses it to respond. Do not include sensitive Ledger records unless necessary for your request.

Policy changes and contact

This policy will be updated when Debts' data practices change. The current published policy will be available through the app and its Google Play listing. For privacy questions, contact sajarinm@gmail.com.
