PERSON-TO-PERSON DEBT TRACKING

Know who owes whom.

Debts keeps each Person's Ledger clear, with money you gave or received, an explainable Balance, and history you can trust.

Core debt tracking works offline, without a Debts account. Record Transfers and Starting Balances in Indian rupees (INR), correct Entries with revision history, record Settlements, and export Statements.

Your data stays under your control.

Choose optional App Lock, save a Backup file, or enable automatic Backup to your own Google Drive. Backup files are not encrypted by Debts. SMS and WhatsApp reminders open drafts that you review and send yourself.

No ads, analytics integration, payment processing, or lending service.

Debts is being prepared for Android closed testing. The application source repository is currently private; this site publishes its product information and privacy policy.
